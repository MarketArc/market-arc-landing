How It Works

Three steps. Any plan. Start tracking prices in under 60 seconds.

🔌

1. Install the extension

One click install on Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. No sign-up needed to browse.

🔍

2. Visit any product page

Open any product on any supported store. The extension reads the price directly from the page — instant and private.

📧

Business plan — 5 minute setup

1. Install the extension — Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. Done in 20 seconds.

2. Track your first product — visit any product page, click the extension, hit Track. It's in your dashboard, checked every hour.

3. Track a competitor — on the same product, click "Track as Competitor" and pick which of your products they compete with. Up to 3 per product.

4. Open your Intelligence Matrix — the Competitors tab shows every competitor, their price, the gap, and who's winning. Export to CSV anytime.

5. Wire up webhooks — go to Settings, drop in your endpoint URL. Every time a competitor moves, you get a real-time ping. Zero delay.

Chrome Web Store compliance

Market Arc's use of information received from the Market Arc browser extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Show data use specifics

Specifically, Market Arc:

  • Uses your email and password only to sign you in and send you price alerts you requested.
  • Uses product URLs only to monitor the prices you chose to track.
  • Does not transfer, sell, or use your data for advertising purposes.
  • Does not monitor your browsing activity — the extension only reads the price on the page when you explicitly click its icon.
  • Does not use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Full privacy policy: app.market-arc.net/privacy. Questions: support@market-arc.net.
